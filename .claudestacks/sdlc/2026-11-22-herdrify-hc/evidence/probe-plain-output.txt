probe-plain: herdr-core plain-mode evidence over `hc connect` (Rust probe, source: evidence/probe-plain/src/main.rs)
Every hc process the probe spawns gets: HC_SESSION=hfy-plain XDG_STATE_HOME=$HFY_D/state XDG_CONFIG_HOME=$HFY_D/config XDG_RUNTIME_DIR=$HFY_D/run, and every inherited HC_* variable (HC_SCOPE, HC_TERMINAL_ID, HC_TERMINAL_SOCKET were set in this shell) removed.
Log format: '-> [conn +ms]' line written to hc connect stdin; '<- [conn +ms]' line read from its stdout (verbatim, minus the trailing 
; lines over 4096 bytes are cut by the probe and say so); '!! [conn +ms]' stderr; 'probe:' the probe's own notes. ms are relative to the probe process start.
